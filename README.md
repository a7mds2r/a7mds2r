## Featured Projects

### [EQCAM-Opt: Energy, Quality & Carbon-Aware Management](https://github.com/a7mds2r/EQCAM-Opt-Energy-Quality-Carbon-Aware-Management)

A simulation-based comparative research framework for analyzing energy, quality, and carbon-aware trade-offs in AI infrastructure.
